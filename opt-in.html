SMS Opt-In & Messaging Terms

CONSEQ accountability notifications · Last updated 2 August 2026

CONSEQ sends one kind of text message, to one kind of person, and only after that person has agreed in writing by replying to us. This page documents exactly how that agreement is obtained.

How you opt in

CONSEQ is a mobile app for people trying to quit a habit. A user of the app names one accountability contact and enters that person's mobile number. That person then receives a single text message asking whether they agree to receive notifications. The call to action is in the message itself, and it is the only message sent unless the recipient agrees.

Message received by the prospective recipient

[Sam] added you as their accountability contact in CONSEQ, an app for quitting a habit. You'd get a text only when they break their own rules — a few times a month at most. Reply YES to agree. Reply STOP to opt out at any time. Msg & data rates may apply.

Replying YES is the opt-in. Nothing further is sent to that number unless and until that reply is received. The reply itself — the verbatim message body, the phone number, the timestamp, and the carrier message ID — is stored as the record of consent.

Automatic reply confirming enrollment

CONSEQ: You're set. You'll only hear from us if the person who added you breaks their own rules — a few times a month at most. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out. Msg & data rates may apply.

If you do nothing, or say no

  • An unanswered invitation expires after 48 hours.
  • A number may be invited a maximum of 3 times, ever.
  • Replying NO or STOP is a permanent refusal. That number is never invited again and can never be messaged.

What we never do

  • We do not send marketing or promotional messages of any kind.
  • We do not include links or phone numbers in messages.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your number with anyone except our messaging provider, who needs it to deliver the message.
  • We do not message anyone who has not replied YES.
