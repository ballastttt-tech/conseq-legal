CONSEQ

An app for restricting your own phone, and for keeping an honest record of how you are doing against your own rules.

The app is free. Everything it holds stays on your own phone: there is no account, nothing is sold, and the app sends nothing anywhere. The only thing that ever reaches us is an email you choose to write.
