CONSEQ

An app for restricting your own phone, and for keeping an honest record of how you are doing against your own rules.

A later version will add an accountability text: if you break the rules you set, one text goes to one person you chose, and only if that person agreed to receive it first. That feature is not switched on in the app available today, which sends no messages to anyone.
