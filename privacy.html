CONSEQ Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 August 2026 · Contact: ballast.ttt@gmail.com · Provider: CONSEQ

CONSEQ helps you restrict your own phone. Nothing you put into the app ever leaves the device you put it on. This policy explains what the app holds and where it holds it.

We collect nothing about you. CONSEQ restricts your phone and keeps your record of it. There is no account, there is no server involved, and the app has no way to send you or your information anywhere. The only thing we ever see is an email you decide to write to us yourself, and there is a section below on exactly what that means.

What stays on your phone

Everything you tell CONSEQ about yourself stays on the device it was typed into. There is no account, no login, and no user profile on any server.

That includes, specifically:

  • Your age, and what you said you wanted back
  • What you told us you had already tried
  • Your answers to the questions the app asks while it is setting itself up for you, and the result they produced
  • Your reason for stopping, in your own words
  • Your clean-day count, your high-risk window, and your full event history, which includes every check in you logged, every panic session you finished or walked away from, and every time you told the app you had broken your own rules
  • Which apps and categories you chose to block, and how much time a day you allowed each of them
  • The date you accepted the terms

Your individual answers to the five questions at the start are never even written to disk. Only the total they add up to, and the group that total falls into, are kept. Two later questions, one about how wrong you think the habit is and one about whether the trouble it causes is more about your values or more about its results, are kept as the numbers you picked, because the app reads them to decide what to say to you next. None of it leaves the device.

Those questions are CONSEQ's own, and the number they produce is only there to decide which words the app shows you next. It is not a test result, not a rating of how bad anything is, and not a diagnosis. Nobody gets diagnosed by an app.

Screen Time and blocking

CONSEQ applies its restrictions through Apple's Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity systems. You grant Screen Time access once, and everything after that happens on your own phone.

Three things happen there. Sites meant for adults are filtered out. The apps and categories you picked are blocked, with whatever daily time you allowed each one. And if you switched it on, apps cannot be deleted from the phone at all while the protocol is running, which is a setting that covers every app and not only the ones you picked.

We cannot see what you browse or which apps you use. Apple deliberately designs that system so apps like this one receive sealed, meaningless identifiers for the things you chose to block. We never learn which apps they are, and we are never told which sites you visited or tried to visit. That information is not available to us at any price.

You can take Screen Time access away at any time in iOS Settings. That clears every restriction CONSEQ applied, including the one on deleting apps.

The camera and the motion sensor

Two of the panic tasks use the camera, and you choose whether to start one.

The breathing task turns on the front camera and shows you your own face for sixty seconds while you breathe. Nothing is attached to that picture at all: no photo, no video, no file, and nothing reading it.

The object task turns on the back camera and asks you to point it at something ordinary and hold it there. Your phone works out what it is looking at using Apple's Vision framework, which runs entirely on the device. Frames are looked at and thrown away in the same instant. No image is saved, and no image is sent anywhere, because the app has nowhere to send one.

iOS asks you for camera permission the first time one of these starts. You can say no. If you do, the other panic tasks still work.

The shake task reads the phone's motion sensor. That needs no permission, and the readings are counted and dropped as they arrive.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, CONSEQ schedules them on the device itself and can show a Live Activity during your high-risk hours. There is no push server behind this app, so nothing you are sent comes from anywhere but your own phone.

If you email us

The Profile tab has a Get support button and a Request a feature button. Both of them open your own Mail app with a message addressed to ballast.ttt@gmail.com, already filled in with the app's version number, its build number and your iOS version. You write the rest, and you decide whether to send it.

If you do send it, we receive your email address and whatever you wrote, the same as with any other email. We read it to answer you and we use it for nothing else. Nothing from inside the app is attached to it, and the app cannot send one on its own.

What leaves your phone

Nothing. The app never contacts a server, and it never hands what it holds to anyone else. There is no analytics kit in it, no crash reporter, and no code from any other company that could send something on its behalf.

The one exception is the one you make yourself. If you tap a support button and then send the email your Mail app opens, that email reaches us, because you sent it. Nothing else ever does.

We never sell what you tell us

We hold nothing about you, so there is nothing for us to sell or to share, and we would not do either in any case. There are no advertising partners, no data brokers, and no other recipients of any kind. That covers support email too: we do not pass it on, and we do not use it to advertise anything to you.

There is no advertising in this app, no analytics kit, and no tracking of any kind.

What we keep, and for how long

Nothing, unless you emailed us. There is no record of you anywhere but your own phone, because the app never sends one. Everything the app holds lives on your device for exactly as long as you keep it there, and goes when you erase it.

Support email is the one thing held anywhere else. We keep those messages in an ordinary mailbox for as long as we need them to answer you, and no longer than 12 months, then we delete them. Ask us to delete yours sooner and we will.

Deleting everything

Inside the app: open the Protocol tab, scroll to The way out, and tap Erase everything and start over. You then hold a button to confirm. That lifts every restriction CONSEQ applied and deletes your protocol, your answers, your reason and your whole event history from the device. The app returns to how it was the first time you opened it.

Because CONSEQ keeps everything on your phone, that one action removes everything the app has. The only thing it cannot reach is an email you sent us, which sits in our mailbox rather than in the app. Write to us and we will delete that too.

Children

CONSEQ is for adults. It is rated 18+ and is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

The app asks your age and will not let you pick a number below 18. That is your own answer and we have no way to check it. If someone under 18 emails us, we delete the message once we know.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export what we hold, and to object to how we use it. We hold nothing about you except an email if you sent one, so the erase option above covers everything in the app, and asking us covers the rest. We will not treat you any differently for asking. Email ballast.ttt@gmail.com with any question about this.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we'll update the date at the top and note the change in the app's release notes.
