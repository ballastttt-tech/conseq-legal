2. What CONSEQ does

You set rules for your own phone. The app restricts the phone accordingly and keeps a record of how you are doing against those rules. Everything it holds stays on that phone.

Using Apple's Screen Time systems, and only with access you grant, the app can:

  • Filter out sites meant for adults, in every browser on the phone.
  • Block apps and categories you pick.
  • Apply a daily time limit you set to any of those apps.
  • Stop apps being deleted, if you switch that on. See section 4.

The rest of the app is a set of tasks to do when you want to ride out an urge, a log of what you did, and a day count. Some of the tasks use the camera or the phone's motion sensor. Nothing is recorded and nothing is sent anywhere. The privacy policy explains that in full.
